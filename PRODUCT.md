# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary users are property teams, architects, interior designers, and private clients evaluating a spatial idea before construction. They need to understand the design, align other decision-makers, approve materials and layouts, or present the project convincingly.

## Product Purpose

VIZ Creative is the public portfolio and enquiry surface for an Abuja-based interior-design and architectural-visualization studio. It must make the work immediately credible, explain how the studio moves an idea toward a real project milestone, and help a prospective client begin a useful conversation.

## Positioning

VIZ Creative combines spatial design and high-fidelity visualization in one process, so design intent and its visual communication stay connected from the first brief through implementation.

## Operating Context

Prospective clients arrive with some combination of drawings, sketches, BIM models, reference images, a desired mood, a site location, a timeline, and a decision or presentation milestone. The studio works across residential, commercial, and hospitality projects in Nigeria and beyond.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Interior design, 3D visualization, virtual reality, space planning, architectural rendering, and turnkey solutions.
- Existing Next.js routes and the current contact channels must remain functional.
- The site must remain responsive and respect reduced-motion preferences.
- The work catalogue currently contains placeholder project data. Until real case studies are supplied, the interface must present the available showreel honestly and must not expose placeholder content.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: VIZ Creative.
- Preserve the existing VIZ media assets and the studio's Abuja location.
- The user wants a modern, premium, image-led presence with restrained display scale, purposeful scrollytelling, high-fidelity text reveals, and an expanding hero frame that keeps the full video composition visible.
- Avoid oversized editorial typography, masked cropping, blurred side fills, and motion used without a narrative purpose.
- Preserve the initial website's overall composition and interaction model; the redesign should primarily establish a more fitting visual theme rather than replace the site's structure.
- Avoid nautical, coastal, instrument-panel, and schematic-analysis motifs. Deep marine blue paired with signal-lime, decorative datum rails, sun-path diagrams, and technical overlays feel distracting from the architectural work.

## Evidence on Hand

- Existing hero and portfolio video files plus one rendered poster image.
- Studio introduction, service descriptions, four-stage process, address, email, phone, and WhatsApp details in the repository data files.
- No verified testimonials, client logos, awards, performance statistics, or finished project case-study records are currently available. Future work must not fabricate them.

## Product Principles

1. Show the work before explaining it.
2. Connect every visual deliverable to a real client decision or project milestone.
3. Make starting a brief feel clear even when the client materials are incomplete.
4. Prefer honest evidence over generic prestige claims.
5. Keep design and visualization presented as one connected practice.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic structure, keyboard-accessible actions, readable contrast, responsive layouts, meaningful media alternatives, and reduced-motion fallbacks.
